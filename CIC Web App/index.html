CIC: Crescent Islamic Club

Established with a vision to nurture faith, knowledge, character and leadership, Crescent Islamic Club has been a platform for students to learn, connect, grow and serve.

  1. Recognized in 2025

    CIC was officially recognized by Crescent University in 2025, a new chapter in its 20+ years of faith, service and impact.

  2. 20+ years of service

    Bringing students together through Islamic learning, educational programs, cultural activities, competitions, leadership opportunities and community initiatives.

  3. From CICS to CIC

    From its beginnings as CICS to its recognition today, CIC keeps empowering students and creating meaningful experiences beyond the classroom.

The Methodology of Practicing Islam

The correct methodology of practicing the deen of Allah (Islam) is to understand and practice it according to the Qur’an, the authentic Sunnah, and the understanding of the Ṣaḥābah and the early generations (the Salaf as-Ṣāliḥ: the Tābiʿūn and Tābiʿ al-Tābiʿīn). There are several strong textual proofs for this.

Qur’an + authentic Sunnah → understood according to the Ṣaḥābah → followed by the early generations

Proofs for this methodology

1Following the ṢaḥābahQur’an 9:100
“The first forerunners among the Muhājirūn and the Anṣār and those who followed them with excellence—Allah is pleased with them…”

The verse praises those who follow the first generations with excellence. This is one of the fundamental principles behind referring to the understanding of the Salaf.

2The Prophet ﷺ praised his generationṢaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2652; Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2533
“The best of people are my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them.”

This establishes the special status of the Ṣaḥābah, Tābiʿūn and Tābiʿ al-Tābiʿīn.

3The “saved group” hadithJāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2641
“That which I and my Companions are upon.”

Narrated by ʿAbdullah ibn ʿAmr (may Allah be pleased with them both). The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said that Banū Isrāʾīl divided into seventy-two sects, and that his Ummah would divide into seventy-three, all of them in the Fire except one. The Companions asked, “Which is that, O Messenger of Allah?” and he answered with the words above. The test of the saved path is therefore following what the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) and his Companions were upon: the Qur’an and the Sunnah as they understood and practised them.

4Warning against newly introduced religious mattersṢaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 2697; Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 1718
“Whoever introduces into this matter of ours that which is not from it, it is rejected.”
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 867
“Every innovation is misguidance.”

This establishes the principle that acts of worship must have evidence from the revelation, rather than being introduced based merely on custom, emotion or personal reasoning.

5Holding firmly to the SunnahSunan Abī Dāwūd 4607; Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī 2676
“Upon you is my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after me. Hold onto it and bite onto it with your molar teeth…”

This is particularly important because the Prophet ﷺ connects his Sunnah with the way of the rightly guided Caliphs.
